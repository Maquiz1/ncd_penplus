How it works. Two background services run next to the website: the scheduler (Celery beat) looks at this list every minute and starts each job at its time, and the worker (Celery worker) does the job: sending SMS, closing LTFU, data checks, the monthly report. Both read the same list, so switching a job off here stops it at the next check.
